# Arrange v0.1.3

Arrange is the safe icon-placement workflow for v0.1.3. The stable path remains normal macOS Command-drag guidance; Assisted Move stays Experimental and explicitly gated.

## Current v0.1.3 Focus

- Clearer Guided Manual Arrange explanation and recovery paths.
- Placement Planner recommendations with reasons and no mutation by default.
- Item preferences for keep visible, hide, always hide, and review later, persisted locally by hashed item key.
- Handoff to Assisted Move dry-run only after the user explicitly chooses the Experimental path, with source, target, direction, and risk called out before any attempt.

## Boundaries

Arrange does not claim stable automated icon moving, bulk moving, or broad third-party activation. Basic arrangement guidance remains usable without Accessibility.

Placement Planner preferences are advisory and privacy-safe by default. They influence future recommendations but do not move menu extras or export raw item titles, bundle identifiers, selected item IDs, or protected names by default.
